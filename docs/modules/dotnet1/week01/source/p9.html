MONDAY STEP 1 · index.html

1 · Sample (run it unchanged)

<!doctype html><html lang="en"><head>  <meta charset="utf-8">  <title>Resume of Juan Dela Cruz</title></head><body>  <h1>Juan Dela Cruz</h1>  <p>Hello! I am Juan Dela Cruz,  a senior high school student in the ICT strand.</p></body></html>

Discussion

  • Look at line 1. <!doctype html> tells the browser that this file is an HTML page. It is always the first line.
  • Look at line 2. <html lang="en"> opens the whole page. Its attribute lang="en" says the page is in English. The page closes on line 12 with </html>.
  • Look at line 4. <meta charset="utf-8"> lets the page show every letter correctly. It has no closing tag.
  • Look at line 5. <title>Resume of Juan Dela Cruz</title> is the tab title. It is the first line of the output, Tab title: Resume of Juan Dela Cruz, but it does not show on the page itself.
  • Look at line 7. <body> starts the part people see. Everything between line 7 and </body> on line 11 is visible page text.
  • Look at line 8. <h1>Juan Dela Cruz</h1> is the main heading: an opening tag, the content and a closing tag. Together they are one element.
  • Look at line 9. <p>Hello! I am Juan Dela Cruz, opens a paragraph with <p>. It goes on to line 10 and closes there with </p>.
  • Look at line 10. a senior high school student in the ICT strand.</p> ends the paragraph. The browser joins lines 9 and 10 into one line of page text.
