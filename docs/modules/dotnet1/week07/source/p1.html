Week 7 starts here: Juan's site is spaced and has a nav bar, but every box sits in the normal flow, one under another: nothing overlaps, nothing sits side by side.
Position, z-index, float and a gallery. position chooses how a box is placed: static (the normal flow), relative (moved from its own place), absolute (placed from its nearest positioned ancestor), fixed (from the window) or sticky (normal until you scroll, then held). top, right, bottom and left give the distances. z-index decides which positioned box is on top. float: left or right moves a box to one side so the next content wraps beside it; clear starts a box below the floats. A gallery is a set of figures, each an img with its figcaption.
ANALOGY A bulletin board: most papers are pinned in rows (the normal flow), a sticky note is stuck on top of one corner (absolute, with a higher z-index), and a photo pinned at the left lets the paper's words run beside it (float).
