Week 02: before you submit

.NET 1 · Multimedia and Tables · Your own resume, your media, the self-check and Week 3

Weeks are linked. CodeShare opens this week only after every Week 1 task is Completed. Your module project and personal performance-task project carry forward from Week 1.
Your own media files. In CodeShare, add your own picture to your personal project under Media files (online only for now). File names may use only letters, numbers, - and _ before the ending, such as my_photo.jpg (no spaces); at most 5 MB each and 10 per project. Module media steps are checked automatically online once your teacher has added the module's media files; offline your teacher presses Mark done.

Before you add your picture

  • ☐ It shows only you, or it is your teacher's picture. No other people.
  • ☐ The name uses only letters, numbers, - and _ before the ending: my_photo.jpg, not my photo.jpg.
  • ☐ The ending is the file's real format (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .gif, .webp or .svg); at most 5 MB.
  • ☐ Online: add it under Media files in your personal project; its src is images/ and its exact name.
  • ☐ Offline: Media files are not available yet; write the img now and add the file online later.
