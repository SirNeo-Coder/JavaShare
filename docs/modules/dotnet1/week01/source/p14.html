Discussion

  • Look at line 28. <li>Sports is a list item that is not closed on this line: its content goes on.
  • Look at line 29. <ul> opens a list inside a list item: a nested list.
  • Look at line 30. <li>Basketball</li> is an item of the inner list, so it shows under Sports.
  • Look at line 32. </ul> closes the inner list.
  • Look at line 33. </li> closes the Sports item, after its inner list.
  • Look at line 34. </ul> closes the outer Interests list from line 24.
  • Look at line 41. <ol start="3" reversed> starts at 3 and, with reversed, counts down: 3, 2, 1. Compare start on line 36.
