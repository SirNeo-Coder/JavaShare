Before you add your states and pseudo-element

  • ☐ Every rule with :hover also has :focus, so the keyboard sees what the mouse sees.
  • ☐ A :focus-visible outline, 3px or more, 3:1 or more against the colour around it; never outline: none on its own.
  • ☐ Colours from the chart: 4.5:1 or more for text.
  • ☐ One colon for a pseudo-class (:first-child), two for a pseudo-element (::before); no space before the colon.
  • ☐ content in quotes, both of them: content: "★ ";. A symbol only: every needed word stays in the HTML.
  • ☐ A symbol before an element's words becomes its first letter: put it in ::after if you also use ::first-letter.
  • ☐ Test with the mouse and with the Tab key in the page window.
