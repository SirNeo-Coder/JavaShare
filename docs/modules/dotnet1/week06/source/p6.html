Five-minute presentation

  1. Introduce yourself and your site (30 seconds).
  2. Press Run code and visit every page with your nav bar (1 minute).
  3. Change one padding, border or margin in styles.css, run again and show the change; then undo it (2 minutes).
  4. Point to your box rules, your nav list rules and your link states (1 minute).
  5. Answer one question (30 seconds).

Prepare for these questions

  1. What is the difference between padding and margin?
  2. Why are spacing and bullets not in the output?
  3. Why must :link and :visited come before :hover?
  4. How do you know your link colours can be read in every state?
  5. Which details did you leave out of your resume to keep it safe?
