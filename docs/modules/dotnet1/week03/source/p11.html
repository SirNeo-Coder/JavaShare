  • Look at line 8 of projects.html. <a href="projects.html">Projects</a> leads to this same page: a link may open its own page.
  • Look at line 8 of projects.html. <a href="contact.html">Contact</a> opens the contact page from Step 2.
  • Look at line 14 of projects.html. </ul> closes Week 1's list of Juan's projects.
