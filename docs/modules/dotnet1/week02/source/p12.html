Discussion

  • Look at line 79. <audio src="audio/greeting.wav" controls></audio> is Tuesday's sound, from Step 3.
  • Look at line 80. <h2>Video introduction</h2> is the video's heading.
  • Look at line 81. <video src="video/juan_intro.webm" uses video to play a short movie. Its file is in the video folder.
  • Look at line 81. controls width="320" shows play buttons and sets the width in pixels, like width on the picture in Step 1.
  • The output ends with Media checks:, one line per picture, sound or video: whether the file loaded. Media are not page text.
