Discussion

  • Look at line 12. 1<sup>st</sup> uses sup (superscript) to raise text, like the st in 1st.
  • Look at line 13. H<sub>2</sub>O uses sub (subscript) to lower text, like the 2 in H2O. The page text still reads H2O.
  • Look at line 14. <b>Favourite tool:</b> uses b to make words bold without saying they are important. Compare strong on line 10, from Step 2.
  • Look at line 15. <i>Bayanihan</i> uses i to set a word apart, such as a word from another language. Compare em on line 11.
  • Look at line 16. <mark>Open to group projects.</mark> uses mark to highlight words, like a highlighter pen.
  • Look at line 17. <small>Sample resume for practice only.</small> uses small for a side note, like fine print.
  • None of these change the page text: the output shows only the words. Each one sits inside a p from Step 1.
