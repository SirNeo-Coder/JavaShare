1 · Sample (run it unchanged; the lines discussed, with their numbers)

…    <tbody>      <tr>        <td>Senior high</td>        <td>Sample Senior High School</td>        <td>11 to 12</td>      </tr>      <tr>        Junior high        Sample Junior High School        7 to 10      </tr>…

Discussion

  • Look at line 66. <tr> starts the Senior high row: its values sit in td cells (lines 67 to 69).
  • Look at line 71. <tr> starts the Junior high row.
  • Look at line 72. Junior high has no td tags. Text in a row but not in a cell does not belong to the table.
  • Look at line 74. 7 to 10 has no td either. The browser moves all three values out of the table, so they show above the caption, on one line.
  • The browser shows no error. Read the page text: words above the caption mean a row lost its cells.
  • The output ends with Media checks:, one line per picture, sound or video: whether the file loaded. Media are not page text.

2 · Guided example

Repair the Junior high row. Replace lines 72 to 74 with these three lines. Then run. The row goes back into the table.

        <td>Junior high</td>
        <td>Sample Junior High School</td>
        <td>7 to 10</td>

3 · Your task (no code given: the guided example is your clue)

Now do it on your own. Keep the cells you repaired in the guided example, but put the rows in time order: move the whole Junior high row above the Senior high row.
