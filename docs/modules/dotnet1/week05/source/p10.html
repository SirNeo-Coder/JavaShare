Before you style your text and header

  • ☐ Every font-family list ends in serif, sans-serif or monospace; a name with spaces is in quotes: "Times New Roman".
  • ☐ Sizes in em multiply the parent's size: 1.1em of 16px is 17.6px.
  • ☐ A gradient goes in background-image, with commas between its parts and # before each colour: linear-gradient(to right, #13305c, #8a1c55).
  • ☐ Text on a gradient reaches 4.5:1 against both colours (chart on page 12); opacity lowers contrast.
  • ☐ Only fonts and colours already on the computer: no @font-face, nothing named in url().
