Discussion

  • Look at line 15 of projects.html. <h2>Send me a message</h2> is a Week 1 heading above the frame. It is page text.
  • Look at line 16 of projects.html. <iframe src="contact.html" shows another page inside this page; src names the page to show.
  • Look at line 16 of projects.html. title="Contact form of Juan Dela Cruz" tells screen readers what the frame holds. It is not page text.
  • Look at line 16 of projects.html. width="420" height="360" set the frame's size in pixels, like img in Week 2.
  • Look at line 16 of projects.html. ></iframe> closes the frame: unlike img, an iframe needs a closing tag.
  • The framed page's text, such as Contact Juan, is not in the output: it belongs to contact.html, not to this page.

2 · Guided example

A frame shows a page inside a page; a link opens it on its own. Put this line on line 17 of projects.html, right after the iframe. Then run, and click the new link. You should get this result.

  <p><a href="contact.html">Open the contact form on its own page</a></p>
