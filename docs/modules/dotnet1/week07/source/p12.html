Before you build your gallery

  • ☐ Each picture is yours or your teacher's, with only you or no people in it; you made it or may use it.
  • ☐ Its name uses only letters, numbers, - and _ before the ending: my_poster.jpg, not my poster.jpg; at most 5 MB, at most 10 files.
  • ☐ Online: add each one under Media files in your personal project; its src is images/ and its exact name.
  • ☐ Each figure holds one img (src, alt, width, height) and one figcaption; the alt says honestly what the picture shows.
  • ☐ Float the figures left with a width in px; every picture the same width and height in px, never %.
  • ☐ The next heading or paragraph has clear: both.
  • ☐ A positioned element needs a parent with position: relative; set top and right (or left) in px, and a z-index.
