TUESDAY Easy to Read: Font Sizes, Spacing and Alignment

Know: Explain how font-size in em, line-height, letter-spacing and text-align make text easier to read.

Value: Appreciate room on a page that helps classmates read without strain.

Do: Space and align Juan's header and size his form labels in em.

Today's starting point: Juan's header has a gradient, but its words sit on the left, close together, and his paragraph lines are tight.
Readable text. font-size can be written in px, in em (times the parent's size: 1.1em of 16px is 17.6px) or in rem (times the page's base size). line-height sets the space each line takes (1.5 is one and a half times the font size); letter-spacing adds space between letters; text-align places lines left, center or right; text-decoration draws or removes lines such as underline. Page checks read sizes in px and colours in rgb().
