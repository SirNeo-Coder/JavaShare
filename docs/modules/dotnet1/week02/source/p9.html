MONDAY STEP 1 · index.html

1 · Sample (run it unchanged; the lines discussed, with their numbers)

…<body>  <h1>Juan Dela Cruz</h1>  <img src="images/juan_profile.svg" alt="Drawing of Juan Dela Cruz smiling" width="160" height="160">  <p>Hello! I am <strong>Juan Dela Cruz</strong>,  a senior high school student in the <em>ICT strand</em>.</p>…

Discussion

  • Look at line 8. <h1>Juan Dela Cruz</h1> is Week 1's heading. The picture goes right under it.
  • Look at line 9. <img src="images/juan_profile.svg" uses img to show a picture. Like br in Week 1, it has no closing tag.
  • Look at line 9. src="images/juan_profile.svg" names the file: the folder images, then the file juan_profile.svg. Every letter and capital must match the uploaded file.
  • Look at line 9. alt="Drawing of Juan Dela Cruz smiling" describes the picture for screen readers, and shows if the picture fails. It is not page text.
  • Look at line 9. width="160" height="160" set the picture's size in pixels.
  • Look at line 10. <p>Hello! I am <strong>Juan Dela Cruz</strong>, is Week 1's introduction, now after the picture.
  • The output ends with Media checks:, one line per picture, sound or video: whether the file loaded. Media are not page text.

2 · Guided example

Juan adds a sentence about his picture that everyone can read. Put this line on line 10, right after the img line. Then run. You should get this result.

  <p>My profile picture is a drawing, not a photo.</p>

3 · Your task (no code given: the guided example is your clue)

Now do it on your own. Change the sentence you added in the guided example so it starts with a label, like a caption: Picture: a drawing of me, not a photo.
