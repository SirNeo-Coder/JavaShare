Pseudo-classes and pseudo-elements. A pseudo-class, written after one colon, picks elements in a state or a place: :hover under the mouse, :focus where the keyboard or a click is, :first-child the first of its siblings. A pseudo-element, written after two colons, styles a part of an element or adds one: ::first-letter its first letter, ::before and ::after a short text before or after its words, given by content.
ANALOGY A class line-up: "the first in line" is whoever stands at the front, and it changes when someone new arrives (a pseudo-class); the big first letter of a name on a certificate is part of the same name (a pseudo-element).
