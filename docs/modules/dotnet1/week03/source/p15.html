Discussion

  • Look at line 8 of contact.html. <a href="contact.html>Contact</a> lost the closing quote after contact.html.
  • Look at line 8 of contact.html. href="contact.html>Contact</a></nav> an attribute value runs from its opening quote to the next quote, so all of this became part of the href.
  • Look at line 9 of contact.html. <h1>Contact Juan</h1> vanished from the output: it is inside the broken href too.
  • Look at line 10 of contact.html. <p>This form is a prototype: it sends nowhere.</p> vanished the same way.
  • Look at line 13 of contact.html. <label for="visitor"> holds the next quote, where the broken href finally ends. That is why Your name joins the navigation line.
