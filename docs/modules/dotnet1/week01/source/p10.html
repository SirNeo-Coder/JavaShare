Discussion

  • Look at line 9. <!-- Add my photo here in Week 2 --> is a comment: a note for whoever reads the code. It starts with <!--, ends with --> and never shows on the page.
  • Look at line 10. <strong>Juan Dela Cruz</strong> marks important words. In Step 1 the name was plain text inside p; now strong sits inside the paragraph.
  • Look at line 11. <em>ICT strand</em> stresses words. Like strong, it changes how the words look, not the page text.
  • Look at line 12. <h2>Skills</h2> is a smaller heading than the h1 on line 8: a section title.
  • Look at line 13. <ul> starts an unordered list: bullets, for items whose order does not matter. It closes on line 16 with </ul>.
  • Look at line 14. <li>Typing documents</li> is one list item. Each li is one line of page text; the bullet is drawn but is not page text.
  • Look at line 18. <ol type="1" start="1"> starts an ordered list: numbered items. The attribute type="1" uses numbers and start="1" starts at 1. The numbers are not page text either.
