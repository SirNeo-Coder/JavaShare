Colours, gradients and opacity. A hex colour is # and six digits (00 to ff for red, green and blue); rgb() gives the same three numbers from 0 to 255, and rgba() adds an alpha from 0 (transparent) to 1 (solid). linear-gradient() runs in a direction (to right; to bottom when none is given) through colours separated by commas; radial-gradient() spreads from the centre. opacity fades the element and everything in it, so its text loses contrast.
ANALOGY Mixing paint for a school mural: two colours blended across a wall make a gradient; adding water thins the paint until the wall shows through, like opacity.
