Discussion

  • Look at line 5. <title>Resume of Juan Dela Cruz</title> is Monday's tab title, the first line of the output.
  • Look at line 9. <!-- Add my photo here in Week 2 --> is Thursday's repaired comment: hidden, as it should be.
  • Look at line 10. <strong>Juan Dela Cruz</strong> is Tuesday's formatting: strong marks important words.
  • Look at line 29. <ul> is Wednesday's nested list inside Interests.
  • Look at line 52. <pre>Day Club is Tuesday's pre, keeping its spaces.
  • Look at line 54. Wednesday Drawing</pre> is the last page text. Anything you add after line 54, before </body>, comes after it in the output.
