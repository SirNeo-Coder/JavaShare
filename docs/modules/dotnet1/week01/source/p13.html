Discussion

  • Look at line 23. <h2>Interests</h2> starts a new section.
  • Look at line 24. <ul> opens a second unordered list, because the order of interests does not matter.
  • Look at line 25. <li>Drawing comics</li> is the first item: it shows first on the page.
  • Look at line 28. </ul> closes the Interests list. An li must sit before this line to belong to the list.
  • Look at line 30. <ol type="A" start="1"> now has type="A", so the goals get letters A and B instead of the numbers of Step 2 (line 18 there had type="1"). Letters are not page text.
