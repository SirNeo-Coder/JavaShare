Discussion

  • Look at line 60. <th scope="col">Level</th> is the first of three header cells (lines 60 to 62), so the table has three columns.
  • Look at line 76. <tr> starts the award row.
  • Look at line 77. <td colspan="3">Award: With honors, Grade 10</td> has colspan="3": one cell covers all three columns.
  • Look at line 78. </tr> ends the row. A row's cells must add up to the table's columns: here 3.
  • Look at line 79. </tbody> closes the data rows.
  • The output ends with Media checks:, one line per picture, sound or video: whether the file loaded. Media are not page text.
