Discussion

  • Look at line 28. <h2>My motto</h2> is a section heading, like Skills in Step 2.
  • Look at line 29. <br> is a line break inside the paragraph. It has no closing tag. Without it the browser joins the lines, as it joined lines 9 and 10 in Step 1.
  • Look at line 32. <hr> draws a line across the page to separate parts. It is not page text.
  • Look at line 34. <pre>Day Club starts pre, which keeps every space and line break exactly as typed.
  • Look at line 35. Monday Chess has Monday (6 letters) and 5 spaces: 11 characters, so Chess starts right under Club.
  • Look at line 36. Wednesday Drawing</pre> has Wednesday (9 letters) and 2 spaces: 11 again. </pre> closes the pre right after the last word.
