contact.html

…  <meta charset="utf-8">  <title>Contact Juan Dela Cruz</title></head><body>  <h1>Contact Juan</h1>  <p>This form is a prototype: it sends nowhere.</p>  <form>    <p>      <label for="visitor">Your name</label><br>      <input type="text" id="visitor" name="visitor">    </p>    <p><button type="submit">Send message</button></p>  </form></body></html>

Discussion

  • Look at line 5 of contact.html. <title>Contact Juan Dela Cruz</title> is the new page's tab title.
  • Look at line 10 of contact.html. <form> holds the boxes and the button. It has no action, so it sends nowhere: it is a prototype.
  • Look at line 12 of contact.html. <label for="visitor">Your name</label><br> is a label. Its words are page text; for="visitor" ties it to the box whose id is visitor. br breaks the line, as in Week 1.
  • Look at line 13 of contact.html. <input type="text" id="visitor" name="visitor"> is a one-line text box. Like img in Week 2 it has no closing tag. What you type in it is not page text.
  • Look at line 13 of contact.html. id="visitor" matches the label's for, so clicking the label puts the cursor in the box; name is the name the form would send with what was typed.
  • Look at line 15 of contact.html. <button type="submit">Send message</button> is the form's button. Its words are page text.
