Performance task · My Resume, Week 1: Personal Info Page · 50 points

Start Thursday; finish and present Friday. In CodeShare open Projects, then Personal performance-task project. Your own resume starts there; it carries forward every week until Week 10.

Build a simple personal info page about yourself: your name as the main heading, a short introduction paragraph, and lists of your skills and interests. Use only safe details: your name, school, strand, skills, interests and achievements. Never put your home address, phone number, birth date, ID numbers or personal social-media accounts on the page.

Use this week's HTML: at least two paragraph formatting elements, a br or an hr, a bullet list and one numbered list that uses type, start or reversed. Run code until the page runs with no error, then press Submit this version. Saving or running is not submitting.

Thursday: 0–60 minutes, repairs and the Thursday strip; 60–120 minutes, plan on paper, show the teacher, then start. Friday: 0–30 minutes, finish and run; 30–105 minutes, five-minute presentations; 105–120 minutes, Friday strip and Submit this version.

Requirements

  • ☐ Keep the doctype, html lang="en", head with meta charset and your own title, and body.
  • ☐ Put your name in one h1.
  • ☐ Write a short introduction paragraph about yourself (safe details only).
  • ☐ Use at least two formatting elements for their meaning, such as strong, em, mark or small.
  • ☐ Use a br inside a paragraph or an hr between two topics.
  • ☐ List at least three skills in a ul.
  • ☐ List at least three interests.
  • ☐ Add one ol (for example, your goals) that uses type, start or reversed.
  • ☐ No home address, phone number, birth date, ID numbers or social-media accounts.
  • ☐ Run code shows no error; press Submit this version, then present.

<!doctype html>
<html lang="en">
<head>
  <meta charset="utf-8">
  <title>My Resume</title>
</head>
<body>
  <!-- Safe details only: no home address, phone, birth date, ID numbers or social media. -->
  <h1>Your Name</h1>
  <p>Write a short introduction about yourself here.</p>
  <h2>Skills</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Write a skill here</li>
  </ul>
  <h2>Interests</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Write an interest here</li>
  </ul>
</body>
</html>

Five-minute presentation

  1. Introduce yourself and your page (30 seconds).
  2. Press Run code and show your page in the page window (1 minute).
  3. Change one ol attribute (type, start or reversed), run, show the markers change, then restore it (2 minutes).
  4. Point to your head, body, one formatting element and one list in the code (1 minute).
  5. Answer one question (30 seconds).

Prepare for these questions

  1. Which part of your page is in head, and which is in body?
  2. Why did you use strong (or em) on those words?
  3. What does your ol attribute change, and why is it not in the page text?
  4. Why are your skills in an unordered list?
  5. Which details did you leave out to stay safe, and why?
