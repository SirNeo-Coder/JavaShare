TUESDAY Hear and See Juan: Adding and Checking Sound and Video

Know: Explain the src, alt, controls and width attributes and how CodeShare checks pictures, sound and video.

Value: Value describing sound and video for classmates who cannot hear or see them.

Do: Add and check Juan's sound greeting and video, then describe each one on the page.

Today's starting point: Monday's page has a picture but no sound or video, and a file's content means nothing to a reader who cannot see or hear it.
Media attributes. src names the file exactly: images/, audio/ or video/, then its name, such as images/juan_profile.svg. controls shows the play button; width and height set the size in pixels; loop plays again from the start; muted starts silent. Text inside audio or video is only for very old browsers, so describe each file in a p.
ANALOGY A barangay announcement played on a speaker also gets a written notice, for people who did not hear it. Sound and video on a page need a written description too.
Media steps: every step this week shows Juan's picture, sound and video, files your teacher adds under Media files. Online, CodeShare checks them automatically; Run code waits on Loading media… until they are on this device. Offline, media cannot load yet: the step shows Checked by your teacher for now, and your teacher presses Mark done.
