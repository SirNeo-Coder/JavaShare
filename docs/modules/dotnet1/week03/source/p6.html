Five-minute presentation

  1. Introduce yourself and your site (30 seconds).
  2. Press Run code, then use only your links to visit every page (1 minute).
  3. Click each label to show that it selects its box, and type in your form (2 minutes).
  4. Point to one href, your iframe's src and title, and one label's for with its id (1 minute).

  3. How does a label know which box it names?
  4. Why does your form send nowhere, and what would a real form need?
  5. Which details did you leave out of your resume to keep it safe?
