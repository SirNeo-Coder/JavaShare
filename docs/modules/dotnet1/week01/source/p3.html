Today's starting point: Monday's page cannot keep a line break or extra spaces: a three-line motto and a lined-up schedule run together.
Paragraph formatting elements. Inside a paragraph, strong marks important words and em stresses words; b and i set words apart without extra importance; mark highlights; small is for side comments; sub and sup lower or raise characters. br breaks a line, hr marks a change of topic, and pre keeps spaces and line breaks exactly as typed.
ANALOGY A teacher's red pen marks what matters in an essay without changing the words. Formatting elements mark words the same way.

  <p>I am in Grade 11, 1<sup>st</sup> semester.
  My science poster was about H<sub>2</sub>O.</p>
  <p><b>Favourite tool:</b> CodeShare.

  <ul>
    <li>Typing documents</li>
    <li>Making slide presentations</li>
  </ul>
  <h2>Goals this term</h2>
  <ol type="1" start="1">
    <li>Build my resume page.</li>
    <li>Help a classmate with HTML.</li>
  </ol>
</body>
</html>
