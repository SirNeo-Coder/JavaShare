TUESDAY Placing and Stacking: Sticky, Z-index and Text Wrap

Know: Explain how top, right, bottom and left place relative, absolute and sticky elements, and which z-index wins.

Value: Appreciate a layout that keeps the navigation reachable for every reader.

Do: Keep Juan's nav on top with sticky and z-index, and wrap his introduction around his floated picture.

Today's starting point: Juan's ribbon and first gallery pictures are placed, but his nav scrolls away, and his picture sits alone above his introduction.
Placing and stacking. relative moves a box but keeps its old space; absolute removes the box from the flow, so the next content moves up. Page checks read top, right, bottom and left as you wrote them in px; a side you did not set reads a px value that follows the window. A higher z-index paints on top, but only on a positioned box: z-index on a static box reads its value and does nothing. A floated picture lets text wrap around it.
ANALOGY A jeepney's signboard stays at the top of the windshield while the road moves past (sticky), and a sticker on the glass is always in front of the board (a higher z-index).
