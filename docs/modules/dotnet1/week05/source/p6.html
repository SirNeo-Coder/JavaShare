Requirements

  • ☐ Keep your Week 4 site: every page, the navigation, the frame, the form and styles.css linked from every page.
  • ☐ Give body one font-family list and your headings another, each ending in serif, sans-serif or monospace.
  • ☐ Set a font-size (in em or px) and a line-height that leaves room between lines.
  • ☐ Use text-align and letter-spacing (or text-decoration) on at least one element.
  • ☐ Choose text colours from the contrast chart: at least 4.5:1 for normal text.
  • ☐ Give your h1 a linear-gradient background, with text at 4.5:1 or more against every gradient colour.
  • ☐ Use opacity once, on a picture or on text that keeps 4.5:1.
  • ☐ Colour never carries meaning alone: the words say it too.
  • ☐ Only fonts and colours already on the computer; nothing in url(). No address, phone, birth date, ID numbers or social media.
  • ☐ Run code shows no error; visit every page; press Submit this version, then present your fonts, colours and contrast.

Five-minute presentation

  1. Introduce yourself and your site (30 seconds).
  2. Press Run code and visit every page with your links (1 minute).
  3. Change one font or colour in styles.css, run again and show the change; then undo it (2 minutes).
  4. Point to your font lists, your gradient rule and one contrast ratio (1 minute).
  5. Answer one question (30 seconds).

Prepare for these questions

  1. Why does every font list end in serif, sans-serif or monospace?
  2. Why are fonts and colours not in the output?
  3. How do you know your header text can be read on every part of the gradient?
  4. What does opacity do to the contrast of text?
  5. Which details did you leave out of your resume to keep it safe?
