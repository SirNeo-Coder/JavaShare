Discussion

  • Look at line 14 of contact.html. placeholder="Type your name" shows a grey hint inside the empty box. It is not page text.
  • Look at line 14 of contact.html. required means the box may not be empty when the form is sent.
  • Look at line 18 of contact.html. <input type="email" id="email" name="email" required> is a box for an email address.
  • Look at line 22 of contact.html. <select id="topic" name="topic"> is a list of choices; the Topic label on line 21 belongs to it.
  • Look at line 23 of contact.html. <option>School project</option> is one choice. Every option's words are page text.
  • Look at line 24 of contact.html. <option>Art club</option> is the last choice, just before </select> on line 25.
  • Look at line 29 of contact.html. <textarea id="message" name="message" rows="4"></textarea> is a box for several lines; rows="4" makes it four lines tall.
  • Look at line 31 of contact.html. <button type="submit">Send message</button> is still the last part of the form.
