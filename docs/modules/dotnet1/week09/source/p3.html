TUESDAY Flexbox Instead of Float: The Gallery and the Nav Bar

Know: Compare float and flexbox for a row of boxes, and say why flex-wrap replaces clear.

Value: Appreciate a simpler way to do what used to need several rules.

Do: Rebuild Juan's gallery and nav bar with flexbox and read what changed in the output.

Today's starting point: Juan's gallery still floats, which needs a margin on every figure and clear: both on every heading; his nav items are inline-block words.
Flexbox instead of float. A float pulls one box out of the normal flow: the boxes after it wrap around it, every figure needs its own margin, and a heading needs clear: both to get below the row. A flex container arranges its items without taking them out of the flow: flex-wrap: wrap moves an item that does not fit onto the next line, gap spaces them, and nothing below needs clear. float is ignored on a flex item.
ANALOGY Water poured around a stone in a stream takes the stone out of the flow and everything bends around it (float); chairs set out in rows for a programme leave the floor plan intact and simply start a new row when one fills up (flex-wrap).

STEP 3 · index.html, styles.css · run page: index.html

Flexbox replaces float. In Week 7 each gallery figure floated left and needed its own margin, with clear: both on the headings. Now .gallery is the flex container: display: flex puts the figures side by side, gap: 12px spaces them, and flex-wrap: wrap moves a figure that does not fit to the next line. The figures keep their width and lose float and margin. Run index.html unchanged.

styles.css · float replaced by flexbox

/* Flexbox instead of float: the figures sit side by side and wrap to the next line when they do not fit */
.gallery {
  display: flex;
  flex-wrap: wrap;
  gap: 12px;
}
.gallery figure {
  width: 170px;
  position: relative;
}
