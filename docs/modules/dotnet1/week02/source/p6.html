Performance task · My Resume, Week 2: Picture and Table · 50 points

Start Thursday; finish and present Friday. In CodeShare open Projects, then Personal performance-task project. Your Week 1 resume carries forward: add this week's parts to it.

Add a picture and a table. The picture is your own photo (optional, with only you in it) or a picture your teacher gives you. Online, add it under Media files in your personal project: in images, with a name of letters, numbers, - and _ only, such as my_photo.jpg, at most 5 MB. Offline, Media files are not available yet: write the img now; the picture shows once the file is added online. Then add an education or skills table with a caption, th headings and at least two rows.

Use only safe details: no home address, phone number, birth date, ID numbers or personal social-media accounts, and no other people in your picture. Run code until the page runs with no error, then press Submit this version. Saving or running is not submitting.

Thursday: 0–60 minutes, repairs and the Thursday strip; 60–120 minutes, plan on paper, show the teacher, then start. Friday: 0–30 minutes, finish and run; 30–105 minutes, five-minute presentations; 105–120 minutes, Friday strip and Submit this version.

Requirements

  • ☐ Keep your Week 1 page: your name in h1, your introduction and your lists.
  • ☐ Add one img with src in images/, an alt that describes it, and width and height.
  • ☐ Use your own photo (only you in it) or your teacher's picture, added under Media files online.
  • ☐ Write a visible sentence that describes your picture.
  • ☐ Add an education or skills table with a caption.
  • ☐ Put the headings in th with scope="col", inside thead.
  • ☐ Add at least two rows of td inside tbody.
  • ☐ Use colspan or rowspan once, for example an award row.
  • ☐ No home address, phone number, birth date, ID numbers or social media; no other people in the picture.
  • ☐ Run code shows no error; press Submit this version, then present.

Five-minute presentation

  1. Introduce yourself and your page (30 seconds).
  2. Press Run code and show your picture and table in the page window (1 minute).
  3. Change one colspan or rowspan, run, show the table change, then restore it (2 minutes).
  4. Point to your img's src and alt, and to your caption and th cells (1 minute).
  5. Answer one question (30 seconds).

Prepare for these questions

  1. Why does your picture need alt text and a visible description?
  2. What must your src match, and where did you add the file?
  3. Which cells are th and which are td, and why?
  4. What does your colspan or rowspan change?
  5. Why did you choose this picture, and is it safe to share?

    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>

      </tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</body>
</html>
