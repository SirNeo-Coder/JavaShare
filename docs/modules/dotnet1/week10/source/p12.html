Before you make your resume responsive

  • ☐ display: grid goes on the container (the box that holds the items), never on the items themselves.
  • ☐ Name your columns: repeat(2, 1fr) for two equal ones, or repeat(auto-fit, minmax(220px, 1fr)) to let the grid count them.
  • ☐ Set the spacing with gap on the container, not a margin on every item.
  • ☐ Every picture and your video: max-width: 100%; and height: auto;. Keep the width and height written in the HTML.
  • ☐ A media query needs its unit: @media (max-width: 900px), never (max-width: 900), and its two closing braces.
  • ☐ Rules inside a query change only how boxes sit. Never add, remove or re-letter a word there: your output must be the same at every width.
  • ☐ Do not guess the width: a column in fr or minmax() is worked out from the space there is, so it is never the same number twice.
  • ☐ Your own project has no checks beside the page, so the page fills the page window and your query may be off. Widen the browser window, then make it narrower, and the page window follows it; it cannot be dragged on its own.
  • ☐ Run code at a wide and at a narrow browser window before you submit.
