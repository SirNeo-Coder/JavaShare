THURSDAY Can Everyone Read It? Contrast and Gradient Repairs, then Start Your Own

Know: Judge whether a text and background pair reaches 4.5:1 and locate a missing bracket or semicolon.

Value: Show patience and respect for every reader when checking contrast.

Do: Repair Juan's broken gradient and unreadable heading, then begin the personal styling.

Today's starting point: Browsers show no error for broken CSS, and a page can have every word right and still be unreadable to the eye.
Readable contrast. Contrast compares the lightness of text and its background, from 1:1 (none) to 21:1 (black on white). Normal text needs at least 4.5:1; large text (24px, or about 19px bold) at least 3:1. On a gradient, check the text against every colour of the gradient. A browser skips a declaration it cannot read and shows no error; a missing ) can switch off every rule after it.
ANALOGY A jeepney signboard with dark letters on a dark board cannot be read at night, however nice the colours; one missing bracket is like tape over the rest of the signboard.
