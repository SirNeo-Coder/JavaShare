Discussion

  • Look at line 8 of contact.html. <nav> gives contact.html the same navigation bar as index.html and projects.html, so every page links to every other page.
  • Look at line 8 of contact.html. <a href="index.html">Home</a> leads from the contact page back to the resume.
  • Look at line 8 of contact.html. <a href="projects.html">Projects</a> leads to the projects page.
  • Look at line 10 of contact.html. <p>This form is a prototype: it sends nowhere.</p> is Step 2's sentence.
  • Look at line 11 of contact.html. <form> is Step 2's form, one line lower because of the navigation bar.
